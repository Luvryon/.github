## 💡 The Story Behind LuviPay

Why are we building a B2B SaaS like LuviPay? **Because we needed it ourselves.**

As the operators behind **Luvora**, we deal with bookings, payments, and customer retention daily. We experienced firsthand the friction of failed payments, last-minute cancellations, and the administrative nightmare of handling disputes. 

We realized that small businesses and travel operators don't need overly complex enterprise software. They need practical, automated tools to:
1. Recover revenue when a customer's payment fails (e.g., via micro-payment splits).
2. Calculate "safe refunds" that protect business margins.
3. Defend against friendly fraud chargebacks without spending hours gathering evidence.

**LuviPay** is our answer. It is the tool we wished we had when we started, now being refined as a standalone solution for other subscription and recurring-revenue businesses.
